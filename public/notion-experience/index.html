This is a working exploration, built around a few clear hypotheses. The next step is seeing what holds up with real people.

FIRST IMPRESSION

Can someone see their own use for Tomo?

Compare the current and proposed hero. Ask people to explain what Tomo does and choose a first action.

Look for clarity, first-action completion, and time to understanding.
PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Does the interface help them move forward?

Test a conversational answer against an editable plan. Observe completion, corrections, and whether the extra structure helps.

Look for task success and confidence—not just clicks.
IDENTITY

Does the little face stay recognizable?

Compare the original and compact mark at small sizes, in notifications, tabs, and light and dark contexts.

Look for recognition and legibility before choosing a direction.
